Sol-1

Sol-1 stands for day one. Then day two.

What it is

A sobriety and habit tracker for Android with a widget that lives on your Pixel lock screen and home screen. The count updates itself, every day, without opening the app.

47days

“Days since” counters with logged resets, daily habits with a check-in button right on the widget, and milestones from day 1 to year 10.

Get the app

Download APK Source on GitHub
  1. Open the release on your phone and download sol-1.apk.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser when Android asks.
  3. Open Sol-1 and add your first tracker.

Put it on the lock screen

  1. Settings → Display & touch → Lock screen → turn on Widgets on lock screen (Android 16 QPR2 or later).
  2. Lock the phone, swipe to the widget page, tap Add, and choose Sol-1.
  3. Long-press the widget to pick which tracker it shows. Add more than one if you like.

The same widget goes on the home screen: long-press the home screen → Widgets → Sol-1.

Private by design

No account, no network, no analytics. Your trackers live only on your phone. The app asks for one permission: to re-arm its midnight refresh after a reboot.
